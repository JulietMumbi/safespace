SafeSpace Community Guidelines

SafeSpace is a space for university students to share experiences, listen to one another and offer peer support in a respectful environment.

1. Treat everyone with respect

Communicate with kindness and empathy. Do not insult, threaten, bully, harass, discriminate against or deliberately humiliate other members.

2. Protect personal information

Do not share your own or another person's full name, phone number, email address, home address, student number or other identifying information in posts and replies. Do not attempt to discover another member's real identity.

3. Respect different experiences

Students have different backgrounds and experiences. Avoid judgement, ridicule or dismissive comments. Offer support without forcing someone to accept your opinions or advice.

4. Share responsibly

Keep discussions relevant to the selected category. Do not post threats, hateful content, explicit harassment, spam or content intended to harm or intimidate another person.

5. Use the reporting feature

If you encounter content that is abusive, harmful or inappropriate, report it for moderator review. Do not retaliate or engage in arguments that may escalate the situation.

6. Understand anonymity and moderation

SafeSpace displays anonymous nicknames to other students. However, this does not mean that the platform collects no account information. Your registered email is used for your account and should not be included in public discussions.

Posts and replies may be checked by automated content filters and reviewed by authorised moderators when reported or flagged. Automated filters may not identify every harmful expression.

7. Understand the purpose of SafeSpace

SafeSpace provides peer support and a place to share experiences. It is not a professional counselling service, medical service or emergency response platform. Peer responses should not replace professional help when it is needed.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, seek help from appropriate emergency services or a qualified professional.

8. Respect community standards

Content that violates these guidelines may be flagged, reviewed, removed or lead to account restrictions in accordance with SafeSpace moderation procedures.
