A SPACE WITH PURPOSE

More Than Just a Discussion Platform

SafeSpace brings together features that encourage meaningful and responsible peer support.

Anonymous Participation

Share your experiences using an anonymous identity without displaying your real name to fellow students.

Organised Discussions

Explore conversations through categories and participate in discussions that relate to your experiences.

A Supportive Community

Listen, respond and connect with other students through respectful and understanding conversations.

Community Moderation

Reporting tools and content moderation help maintain a respectful environment for everyone.

A NOTE ON YOUR WELLBEING

Your wellbeing matters.

SafeSpace is a peer-support platform where students can share experiences and encourage one another. It is not a substitute for professional mental health care, counselling or emergency support.
